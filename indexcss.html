Profile — Sara Tendulkar

Sara Tendulkar was born on 12 October 1997 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, and is the elder child of legendary cricketer Sachin Tendulkar and pediatrician Anjali Tendulkar. She grew up in a family that values education, discipline, and social responsibility. Although her father is one of the most celebrated cricketers in history, Sara chose a different path and focused on academics and health sciences. She completed her schooling at Dhirubhai Ambani International School in Mumbai and later moved to London to pursue higher education at University College London (UCL). She earned a degree in Biomedical Sciences and subsequently completed a master's degree in Clinical and Public Health Nutrition, demonstrating her strong interest in health, nutrition, and scientific research. Sara enjoys traveling, reading, fitness activities, and exploring different cuisines. She is passionate about promoting wellness and encouraging people to adopt healthier lifestyles through proper nutrition and self-care. Over the years, she has become a role model for many young individuals who admire her ability to balance education, professional success, and social responsibility. While many people initially recognized her as the daughter of Sachin Tendulkar,Sara has gradually established her own identity as a qualified nutritionist,model,philanthropist, and influential public personality.Her journey reflects dedication,hard work,and a commitment to creating a positive impact beyond the world of cricket, making her one of the most admired young personalities in contemporary India.

Profile — Sara Arjun


Sara Arjun is one of the most recognized child actors in Indian cinema and has successfully transitioned into young-adult roles. Born on 18 June 2005 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, she entered the entertainment industry at a very young age. Her father, Raj Arjun, is a well-known actor has appeared in numerous Hindi and regional films.Sara began her career with television advertisement and reportedly featured in more than a hundred commercials before making her film debut. Her expressive face, confidence in front of the camera, and ability to portray emotions naturally made her a favorite among directors and audiences. As she grew older, Sara continued balancing her education with acting assignments. She became known as one of India's highest-paid child artists due to her popularity and consistent success. In recent years, she has started taking on more mature roles, reflecting her transition from childstar to leading actress. One of the biggest milestones in her career came when she was cast in Dhurandhar,where she was reported to play a significant role oppositeRanveer Singh.The announcement generated considerable attention because it marked her emergence as an adult actress in mainstream Bollywood. Apart from acting, Sara is admired for her dedication, professionalism, and grounded personality.She enjoys dancing, traveling, and spending time with family.Despite achieving fame at a young age, she has maintained a relatively private life and focused on developing her skills as a performer.
